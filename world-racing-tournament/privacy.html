Privacy Policy for World Racing Tournament

Effective 22 October 2026

World Racing Tournament ("the game") is developed and published by Midnight Lode ("we", "us"). It is not released yet; this policy describes the game as it will be at release. It explains what information the game uses, who uses it, and the choices you have.

What we collect ourselves

Nothing that identifies you. The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat, and we never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. Your progress (coins, cars, upgrades, paint, settings and best scores) is saved only on your device. We do not receive it, and it is deleted permanently when you uninstall the game.

In-app purchases

The game may offer optional purchases, such as coin packs. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your card or other payment details; we only learn that a purchase was made, so the game can deliver it.

Advertising

The game is free and is paid for by ads. Ads you watch are always your choice: you tap a button that offers a reward, such as extra coins. The game may also show ads inside the game world, for example on roadside billboards. To show ads, measure them and limit how often you see the same one, the ad companies below may collect information from your device:

  • your device's advertising ID (on Android, the Google Advertising ID)
  • your IP address and the rough location it suggests (country or city, not your exact location)
  • device details: model, operating system, language, screen size and network type
  • ad activity: which ads were shown, how long they were visible, and whether they were watched or tapped

Each company handles this information under its own privacy policy:

We do not receive any of this information in a form that identifies you; we see only totals, such as how many ads were shown and what they earned.

Personalised ads

Before the game uses your advertising ID to choose ads that are more relevant to you, it asks for your permission. Until you answer, we treat your answer as no. If you say no, you are shown ads that are not personalised. The game is the same either way: the same features, the same rewards and the same number of ads.

Your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: on Android, open Settings, then Privacy (or Google), then Ads. Deleting it is supported on Android 12 and later. You will still see ads, but they will not be based on your interests.
  • Do not watch ads: every ad you watch is optional, and the game can be played without watching any.
  • Delete your data: uninstall the game to remove everything saved on your device. To ask an ad company about data it holds, contact it through the policy linked above, or write to us and we will help.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has used the game and an ad company has collected their information, contact us and we will help get it removed.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR (Europe and the UK) and the CCPA (California) may give you the right to see, correct or delete information about you, and to object to how it is used. Since we hold no personal information ourselves, most requests are for the ad companies above, but you can always start by writing to us.

Security

Information sent between the game and the ad companies travels over encrypted connections. No method of sending data over the internet is completely secure, but we only work with companies that take reasonable measures to protect it.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts using information in a new way, we will update this page and change the date at the top.
