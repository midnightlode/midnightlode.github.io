Privacy Policy for World Racing Tournament

Effective 14 November 2026

World Racing Tournament ("the game") is developed and published by Midnight Lode ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, who uses it, and the choices you have.

What we collect ourselves

Only what is needed to keep your progress safe and to let you race other players. The game has no chat, and we never ask for your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. The only thing you type in is a player name of your own choosing, which other players see (see "Online races and your player name" below).

Your game progress and account

Your progress (coins, cars, upgrades, paint, player level, season, daily challenges, race cards, settings, best scores, your recent races and the record of your purchases) is saved on your device and also stored by Unity Cloud Save, a service of Unity Technologies, so it is not lost if you change or reset your phone. It is stored under a player ID that Unity Authentication makes for you; this ID is not your name and does not identify you by itself.

On Android the game signs you in with Google Play Games when it can, so your progress can come back on another phone. Google then gives the game your Play Games player ID and your gamer name, which the game shows on its Account page. The game does not receive your email address or your Google password. You can play without Google Play Games; your progress is then kept under your player ID only.

To keep the game fair, changes to your coins, cars, upgrades, crates and race cards are also checked by our own game rules running on Unity's servers (Unity Cloud Code), under the same player ID. The game also asks Unity's servers for a few of its own settings, such as whether online races are open (Unity Remote Config); this request carries your player ID and the game's version.

Online races and your player name

The game lets you race other players online, either with players found for you or with friends who join your race with a code. These races use Unity Multiplayer Services, which makes the race rooms, and Unity Relay, which connects the phones in a race through Unity's servers so that other players never learn your IP address.

While you are in a race room or a race, the other players in it see your player name, the car you drive, its paint and its performance rating, and their phones receive your car's position and speed many times a second so that it can be shown on their screens. A room and what was sent in it are not kept after the room closes. Players you race may see your player name in the list of their recent races on their own phones.

You choose your player name in the game and can change it. Please do not use your real name or anything that identifies you. If another player cannot be heard during a race, the game briefly contacts Unity's sign-in server to check whether your own phone is still online; this sends nothing about you.

In-app purchases

The game may offer optional purchases, such as coin packs. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your card or other payment details; we only learn that a purchase was made, and its order number, so the game can deliver it once and never twice. That order number is kept with your progress.

Advertising

The game is free and is paid for by ads. Ads you watch are always your choice: you tap a button that offers a reward, such as extra coins. To show ads, measure them and limit how often you see the same one, the ad companies below may collect information from your device:

  • your device's advertising ID (on Android, the Google Advertising ID)
  • your IP address and the rough location it suggests (country or city, not your exact location)
  • device details: model, operating system, language, screen size and network type
  • ad activity: which ads were shown, how long they were visible, and whether they were watched or tapped

Personalised ads

In the UK, the European Economic Area and Switzerland, the game asks for your permission, using a form from Google, before your advertising ID is used to choose ads that are more relevant to you. Until you answer, we treat your answer as no. If you say no, you are shown ads that are not personalised.

In the United States, ads may be personalised unless you opt out. Residents of states with privacy laws, such as California, can choose "Do not sell or share my personal information" at any time under Settings, Privacy Choices. If you opt out, we tell our ad companies not to use your information for personalised ads.

You can change your answer at any time under Settings, Privacy Choices. Your answer is kept only on your device. The game is the same either way: the same features, the same rewards and the same number of ads.

Your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: on Android, open Settings, then Privacy (or Google), then Ads. Deleting it is supported on Android 12 and later. You will still see ads, but they will not be based on your interests.
  • Change your ad choices: open Settings in the game and tap Privacy Choices (shown where the law gives you this choice).
  • Do not watch ads: every ad you watch is optional, and the game can be played without watching any.
  • Delete your data: in the game, open Settings, then Account, then Delete My Data. This deletes your progress and your player name from your device and from Unity's servers, and deletes your player ID, at once. You can also ask us by email; see how to delete your data. Uninstalling the game removes only the copy on your device. To ask an ad company about data it holds, contact it through the policy linked above, or write to us and we will help.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Player names are shown to other players in online races, so children should not use the game. If you believe a child has used the game and an ad company has collected their information, contact us and we will help get it removed.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR (Europe and the UK) and the CCPA (California) may give you the right to see, correct or delete information about you, to object to how it is used, and to opt out of its sale or sharing for personalised ads. The only information kept for us is your game progress, your player name and your player ID (above), which you can delete yourself in the game; for anything else, start by writing to us.

Security

Information sent between the game and the companies above travels over encrypted connections. No method of sending data over the internet is completely secure, but we only work with companies that take reasonable measures to protect it.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts using information in a new way, we will update this page and change the date at the top.
