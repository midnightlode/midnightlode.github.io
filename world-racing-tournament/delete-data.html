Delete your World Racing Tournament data

Updated 15 November 2026

World Racing Tournament is made by Midnight Lode. You can delete your account and everything the game keeps about you at any time, in the game or by email.

In the game

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Account.
  3. Tap Delete My Data and confirm.

This deletes, at once, your progress on your phone and on our servers (Unity Cloud Save), your player name and your player ID, and with your player ID what Unity's services keep for it, such as the server's check of your coins. The game then starts again as a new player.

Without the game

If you no longer have the game installed, email us and ask for your data to be deleted. Send it from the Google account you play with, or include the Player ID shown on the game's Account page, so we can find your data. We delete it within 30 days and reply when it is done.

midnightlode@gmail.com

What is deleted

  • Your saved progress: cars, coins, upgrades, paint, player level, season, daily challenges, race cards, settings, best scores and your recent races
  • Your player name
  • The record of your purchases kept with your progress
  • Your player ID

What is kept

Records of the online races you took part in (race ID, player names and IDs, cars, times and places), kept only to look into problems with races, are deleted automatically 30 days after each race. To have them deleted sooner, email us with your Player ID. Nothing else is kept by us. Google Play keeps its own record of your purchases under Google's policies, and your Google Play Games profile stays yours on Google's side. Ad companies hold their own data; see our privacy policy for how to reach them.
