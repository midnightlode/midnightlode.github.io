Privacy Policy for Sikander: The Conqueror

Effective 3 November 2026

Sikander: The Conqueror ("the game") is developed and published by Midnight Lode ("we", "us"). It is not released yet; this policy describes the game as it will be at release. It explains what information the game uses, who uses it, and the choices you have.

What we collect ourselves

Nothing that identifies you. The game has no accounts and no chat, and we never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. The daily sign-in is a daily reward saved on your device, not an account. Your progress (coins, gems, upgrade cards, chapters and stars won, settings and the match in progress) is saved only on your device. We do not receive it, and it is deleted permanently when you uninstall the game.

In-app purchases

The game offers optional purchases, such as packs of gems, a piggy bank, a starter pack, a season pass and a one-time purchase that removes the ads shown between matches. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play, through Unity's purchasing service. We never see or store your card or other payment details; we only learn that a purchase was made, so the game can deliver it. Gems can also be earned by playing.

Advertising

The game is free and is paid for by ads. Some ads you choose to watch: you tap a button that offers a reward, such as free gems. The game also shows an ad between matches now and then; the purchase that removes ads stops those. To show ads, measure them and limit how often you see the same one, Unity Ads, a service of Unity Technologies, may collect information from your device:

  • your device's advertising ID (on Android, the Google Advertising ID)
  • your IP address and the rough location it suggests (country or city, not your exact location)
  • device details: model, operating system, language, screen size and network type
  • ad activity: which ads were shown, how long they were visible, and whether they were watched or tapped

Unity handles this information under its own privacy policy, which you can read at unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy. Unity's purchasing service, which the game starts so that you can buy things, is part of the same company and follows the same policy. We do not receive any of this information in a form that identifies you; we see only totals, such as how many ads were shown and what they earned.

Personalised ads

Before the game is released it will ask, the first time you open it, whether your advertising ID may be used to choose ads that are more relevant to you. Until you answer, we will treat your answer as no. If you say no, you will be shown ads that are not personalised. The game will be the same either way: the same features and the same rewards.

Your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: on Android, open Settings, then Privacy (or Google), then Ads. Deleting it is supported on Android 12 and later. You will still see ads, but they will not be based on your interests.
  • Do not watch ads: the ads that offer a reward are optional, and the game can be played without watching any of them.
  • Stop the ads between matches: buy the one-time purchase that removes them.
  • Delete your data: uninstall the game to remove everything saved on your device. To ask Unity about data it holds, contact it through the policy linked above, or write to us and we will help.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has used the game and an ad company has collected their information, contact us and we will help get it removed.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR (Europe and the UK) and the CCPA (California) may give you the right to see, correct or delete information about you, and to object to how it is used. Since we hold no personal information ourselves, most requests are for Unity, but you can always start by writing to us.

Security

Information sent between the game and Unity travels over encrypted connections. No method of sending data over the internet is completely secure, but we only work with companies that take reasonable measures to protect it.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts using information in a new way, for example by adding another ad company or analytics, we will update this page and change the date at the top.
