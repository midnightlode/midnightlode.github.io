Sikandar: World Conquest

Lead history’s greatest conquerors across a real globe of 1,500 states and provinces. Begin as Sikandar, Alexander the Great, with a single province in Pella. Drag from your land to a neighbour to send your troops, take it, and keep going.

  • 1,500 states
  • 190 countries
  • 23 leaders
  • 270+ chapters
  • World Conquest
Coming soon to Google Play Android, portrait. Free to play, with ads and optional purchases.

What you'll do

Conquer a real globe

1,500 territories in 190 countries, with sea routes, rivers, mountains and deserts.

Fast real-time battles

Every soldier who arrives removes one defender. Outnumber, outflank, overwhelm.

World Conquest

Seven powers, one planet. Choose your leader from 23, among them Sikandar, Cleopatra, Attila, Saladin, Genghis Khan, Suleiman and Napoleon, and start from their homeland against the empires of their own age.

An epic campaign

Over 270 story chapters, from Philip of Macedon to Napoleon’s last campaigns. Choose your general after Sikandar’s death and your side in the age of the crusades, then 1,000 more missions in the Long War.

Command, don’t just click

Heroes who lead from the front, forts, harbours and trained troops, wonders of the ancient world, and skills: Rally, Shield Wall, Thunderbolt, Rout and one special to every campaign.

Rivals with character

Some attack, some dig in, some come by sea, and they make pacts and coalitions against you. Then come revolts, plagues, storms, pirates and hard winters.

On the map

Drag from your land to a neighbour to send 25, 50, 75 or 100% of your troops. Collect and upgrade cards up to level 50, with hero levels, daily quests, a daily challenge, a weekly event and a season pass. Your World Conquest is saved when you leave.
