Real-time conquest on the globe

Sikander:The Conqueror

You are Sikander, Alexander the Great, and the whole globe is your map. Start in Pella with one province and an army. Drag from your land to a neighbour to send troops, take it, and keep going.

  • 1,500 states
  • 190 countries
  • 8 campaigns
  • 118 chapters
  • World Conquest
Coming soon to Google Play Android, portrait. Free to play, with ads and optional purchases.

What you'll do

Conquer a real globe

1,500 states and provinces in 190 countries, with sea routes across the water.

Fast real-time battles

Every soldier that arrives removes one defender. Outnumber, outflank, overwhelm.

Eight campaigns

Play Philip, Sikander, Seleucus, Antiochus, Mithridates, Cleopatra, Ptolemy and Antigonus, with “what if” chapters in lands they never reached.

World Conquest

Macedon against Persia, Maurya, Rome, Carthage, Qin and the Maya, for the whole planet.

Heroes, forts and skills

Move your hero to the front and his land grows faster and strikes harder. Fortify the cities that matter, and call on four skills in battle.

Rivals with character

Rome attacks, Maurya digs in, Carthage comes by sea, and all of them unite against whoever runs away with the map.

On the map

Drag from your land to a neighbour to send 25, 50, 75 or 100% of your troops. Upgrade cards, daily rewards and stars in every chapter give you reasons to come back.
