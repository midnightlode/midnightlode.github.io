Tap Miner

Swing your pickaxe, crack open the rock, and build a mining empire one tap at a time. Dig from a single coal seam down through Iron, Copper, Silver, Gold, Diamond and beyond, all the way to the Antimatter Reactor.

Hire a crew of eleven miners, each with a speciality, and put them to work in any mine you have opened. Collect 380 relics for the Hall of Fame, finish three daily contracts, and keep earning while the game is closed.

  • 10 mines
  • 11 crew
  • 380 relics
  • Plays offline
  • Every ad optional
