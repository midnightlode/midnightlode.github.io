What you'll do

Tap to mine

Chain taps into a 5× combo, land 10× critical hits, and hit the glowing weak points in the rock for a huge payout. A Gold Rush now and then triples everything you earn for fifteen seconds.

Hire your crew

Eleven miners, each with a speciality, can work in any mine you have opened. They dig while you tap and while you sleep.

Collect 380 relics

Rare relics drop as you mine. Common, Rare and Legendary, and finishing a mine's set earns a permanent +25% bonus.

Daily contracts

Three new jobs every day, drawn from over thirty. Finish all three to earn a gem.

Weekend events

Every weekend brings a new event: double relics, gold rush weekends, lucky strikes and gem bonanzas.

Goals and rewards

Over 150 lifetime goals in the Hall of Achievement, and daily rewards that grow across a seven-day streak.

A crew with specialities

Mines only hold a few workers each, so where you put them is a real decision.

Grafter

Digs faster than anyone.

Prospector

Turns up far more relics in his mine.

Night shift

Earns more while you are away.

Foreman

Speeds up every other worker beside him.

Lucky

Raises your critical hit chance while he works.

Play your way

Earns while you sleep

Your crew keeps working while the game is closed. Come back to a pile of coins.

Plays offline

No account, no sign-up, no internet required. Your progress is saved on your device.

Every ad optional

Ads reward you for watching, never interrupt your mining, and the whole game can be finished without one. In-app purchases are available.
